Attachly — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 July 2026 · Published by Attachly (attachlyapp.com)

Attachly (“the add-on”, “we”) is an add-on for Google Sheets™ that attaches files from Google Drive™ to spreadsheet cells. This policy describes the data Attachly accesses and how it is used.

1. Data we access

With your permission via Google sign-in, Attachly accesses only: (a) files you explicitly upload or select with Attachly (Drive drive.file scope — we cannot see the rest of your Drive); (b) the spreadsheet the add-on is open in, to insert file links into cells you choose; (c) your add-on settings (such as your chosen upload folder). We do not access your email, contacts, or any other Google data.

2. How we use it

Solely to provide the features you invoke: uploading files to your Google Drive and inserting links into cells you choose. Nothing else.

3. Storage & transmission

Your files are stored only in your own Google Drive. Settings are stored with your Google account via Google's Apps Script property storage. Attachly runs entirely on Google's infrastructure — we operate no external servers, and your file contents and spreadsheet data are never transmitted to us or to any third party.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any user data. Attachly contains no ads and no profiling.

5. Limited Use disclosure

Attachly's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Deleting your data

Uninstall Attachly and revoke its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored settings can be deleted on request. Files uploaded to your Drive are yours and remain unaffected.

7. Changes & contact

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date. Questions or deletion requests: attachlyapp@gmail.com.
